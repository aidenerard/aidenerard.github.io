Hi, I'm Aiden Erard.

I'm a Computer Engineering student at Georgia Tech. I chose Computer Engineering because I didn't want to choose between hardware and software. I wanted to understand both.

Most of my time outside class goes into personal projects, from autonomous drones to power monitoring systems to AI-powered apps. The one I've invested the most in is Verus, an AI-powered platform for subsurface infrastructure inspection that I hope to keep building long into the future.

I'm still figuring out exactly where to focus, whether that's robotics, embedded systems, or autonomous vehicles, but I want to keep building things that are useful and keep pushing myself technically. Read more about me →
